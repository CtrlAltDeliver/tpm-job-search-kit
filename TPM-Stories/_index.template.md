## Coverage checklist (every strong story should surface these)

1. Impact  2. Key decision  3. Approach chosen + why  4. Alternative *not* chosen
5. Tradeoffs  6. Stakeholders influenced  7. Moments of tension
8. Driving alignment under disagreement  9. Process/roadmap/strategy changes
10. What you'd do differently

Most people are thinnest on **#7 (tension)** and **#8 (alignment under
disagreement)** — build at least one story that nails both.
