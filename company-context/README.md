# company-context/

One markdown file per company you're researching or interviewing with, named by
kebab-case slug (e.g. `shopify.md`, `smart-technologies.md`).

Both routines read these:
- `/apply` cross-references them when scoring.
- `/interview-prep` reads them heavily — company priorities, culture, reported
  interview questions, and the interviewer read.

A good company-context file has:
- **Company snapshot** — what they do, business model, recent news.
- **The role** — what this specific req is looking for.
- **Interview process** — rounds, format, known question styles (Glassdoor,
  levels.fyi, Blind, Prepfully).
- **## Typical interview questions** — real reported questions, grouped by round.
- **Culture & values** — anything that should shape behavioral answers.
- **## Interviewer(s)** — filled in by `/interview-prep` when it looks the
  interviewer up.

You can build one by hand, or ask the AI to research a company and write it.
